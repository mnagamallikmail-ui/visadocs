Privacy Policy & Personal Data Governance

Effective Date: October 2026 Policy Version: v2026.1 (DPDP Aligned) Data Fiduciary: Pro Valuer OPC Pvt Ltd
Notice to Data Principals: This policy is issued under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 (India) and governing rules. ProValuer acts as a Data Fiduciary regarding the personal data of clients, property owners, and authorized representatives.

1. Identity of the Data Fiduciary

Pro Valuer OPC Pvt Ltd ("Pro Valuer", "we", "us", or "our") is a specialized property valuation practice and corporate advisory firm governed by the Companies Act, 2013, the Wealth Tax Act, 1957 (Section 34AB), and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) valuation framework.

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

To deliver certified appraisal reports and satisfy statutory requirements, we collect and process:

  • Identity & KYC Information: Full name, corporate title, email address, mobile phone number, Permanent Account Number (PAN), and GST registration details.
  • Property & Title Documentation: Registered title deeds, sale deeds, link documents, encumbrance certificates (EC), land conversion orders, building permission sanctions, property tax receipts, and site boundary coordinates.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: Invoicing data, quotation approvals, bank UTR transfer numbers, and payment receipt verifications.
  • Technical Telemetry: Log records of portal access, including client IP addresses, browser user agent strings, and timestamped actions recorded in our statutory immutable ledger.

3. Lawful Grounds and Specified Purposes for Processing

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023, data is processed solely for lawful purposes on the basis of your explicit, affirmative consent or for legitimate uses recognized under Section 7:

  • Valuation Mandate Execution: Conducting desk analysis, circle rate verification, physical site survey scheduling, and generating authenticated appraisal certificates.
  • Statutory Compliance & Tax Accounting: Fulfilling mandatory book-keeping, GST tax invoicing, and audit obligations under Indian corporate and taxation laws.
  • Operational Communications: Issuing quotation notices, report delivery notifications, and critical mandate updates via SMS, email, and secure portal alerts.

4. Data Principal Rights Under Chapter III

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you enjoy statutory rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to Access Information (Section 11): Request a summary of your personal data being processed and the identities of any authorized processors with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12): Request correction of inaccurate personal data, completion of incomplete data, or erasure of data that is no longer necessary for the specified purpose.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Redressal of any grievance through our designated Data Protection Officer within 7 working days.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Nominate an individual to exercise rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4)): Withdraw consent at any time via your Client Profile dashboard or written notice. Withdrawal operates prospectively and does not invalidate prior lawful processing.

5. Data Retention & Secure Disposal

Valuation dossiers and accompanying title deeds are retained for a statutory audit window of 8 years from the date of certificate issuance, conforming to Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 11UA of the Income Tax Rules. Uncompleted drafts or abandoned inquiries are permanently purged after 90 days.

6. Technical Safeguards & Encryption Standards

All personal data at rest is secured using AES-256 encryption. Data in transit across all public endpoints is strictly transmitted over TLS 1.3. Access within ProValuer is governed by strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC), and administrative queries are recorded in tamper-evident audit logs.
