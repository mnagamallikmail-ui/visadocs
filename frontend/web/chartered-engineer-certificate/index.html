Primary Statutory Use Cases for Chartered Engineer Certificates

Chartered Engineer Certificate for Imports & Customs (SVB)

Under Circular No. 25/2015-Customs and Foreign Trade Policy provisions, second-hand capital goods and imported used plant equipment imported into India require physical inspection and valuation by a Chartered Engineer. We verify the country of origin, year of manufacture, current operational status, replacement value, and certify realistic depreciated CIF values to prevent customs detention and dispute SVB enhancements.

Chartered Engineer Certificate for Visa & Financial Solvency

Foreign consulates (US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Schengen) mandate verifiable net worth documentation. Our Chartered Engineers conduct structural surveys of residential flats, independent houses, commercial plots, and industrial land across Hyderabad, issuing certified technical evaluation certificates verifying plinth area, market rate, and structural asset stability.

DGFT EPCG & Advance Authorization Installation Certificates

Under the Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG) scheme and Advance Authorizations, Indian manufacturing exporters importing capital machinery at zero or concessional customs duty must submit a Chartered Engineer Installation Certificate within 6 months to certify that the machinery has been physically installed and commissioned at the factory premises.

Frequently Asked Questions — Chartered Engineer Certificate

What is a Chartered Engineer Certificate?

A Chartered Engineer Certificate is a statutory technical document issued by a corporate member of the Institution of Engineers (India) [IEI]. It certifies technical specifications, equipment operational condition, remaining useful life, and financial valuation for government regulators, customs authorities, banks, and foreign consulates.

Why is a Chartered Engineer Certificate mandatory for imported second-hand machinery?

Under Indian Customs regulations and DGFT Foreign Trade Policy, imported used/refurbished capital machinery requires a Chartered Engineer Certificate to inspect the country of origin, verify year of manufacture, quantify depreciated CIF value, and confirm remaining economic life for duty assessment under Special Valuation Branch (SVB) norms.

How is a Chartered Engineer Certificate utilized for visa applications?

For student visas (US F-1, Canada, UK, Australia) and investor/PR visas, a Chartered Engineer evaluates residential, commercial, or industrial real estate properties, certifying plinth area, structural construction specifications, and physical valuation as unassailable financial solvency proof.

What documents are required to obtain a Chartered Engineer Certificate?

Key documents include: machinery commercial invoices, bill of lading / packing lists, manufacturer technical datasheets, plant layout drawings, run-hour logs, and photos of machine rating plates (for machinery); or registered sale deeds, sanction plans, and link documents (for property).

What is the turnaround time for issuing a Chartered Engineer Certificate in Hyderabad?

Standard certificates are issued within 24 to 48 hours following physical technical inspection or submission of complete verification dossiers. Priority same-day service is available for customs clearance deadlines and urgent embassy appointments.
