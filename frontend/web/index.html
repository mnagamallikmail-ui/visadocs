1. Banking Valuation & Credit Collateral Advisory

In the institutional banking sector, asset valuation serves as the foundational safeguard against non-performing assets (NPAs) and regulatory capital impairment. Pro Valuer delivers institutional-grade collateral appraisal services to Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) banks, private commercial banks, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), and housing finance corporations (HFCs) under strict adherence to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Directions and SARFAESI Act statutory frameworks.

Prudential Collateral Appraisals & Credit Exposure

Every commercial credit facility, corporate consortium loan, or structured debt underwriting requires meticulous verification of the underlying primary and collateral security. ProValuer Commercial conducts exhaustive title-to-ground physical verifications, municipal sanction map verifications, and market absorption analyses. Our bank valuation reports establish three critical values for credit committee consideration:

  • Fair Market Value (FMV): The estimated price at which an asset would exchange between a willing buyer and a willing seller in an arm's-length transaction, after proper marketing wherein the parties had each acted knowledgeably, prudently, and without compulsion.
  • Realizable Value: The realistic price anticipated under standard market marketing timelines (typically 90 to 180 days), taking into account prevailing liquidity constraints, micro-market absorption velocity, and transaction encumbrances.
  • Distressed / Forced Sale Value (FSV): The discounted recovery value achievable under immediate liquidation or restricted marketing conditions, essential for determining haircut margins and SARFAESI reserve prices.

Consortium Lending & Multiple Banking Mandates

For mega-infrastructure initiatives, manufacturing conglomerates, and commercial real estate syndications involving multiple scheduled commercial lenders, Pro Valuer acts as the neutral, empanelled consortium valuer. We reconcile conflicting branch estimates, verify title deeds, assess shared charges with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) on the CERSAI portal, and formulate unified techno-economic valuation memoranda accepted unanimously by Joint Lenders' Forums (JLF).

Lenders' Independent Engineer (LIE) & Project Monitoring

Beyond static point-in-time valuation, ProValuer Commercial provides comprehensive Lenders' Independent Engineer (LIE) services for greenfield and brownfield infrastructure developments. Our engineering teams review technical feasibility, project cost estimates, regulatory approvals, contractor milestones, bill of quantities (BOQ), and physical construction velocity prior to recommending credit tranche disbursements.

2. Corporate Valuation & Equity Intelligence

Corporate transactions, regulatory capital reorganizations, and cross-border cross-holdings demand flawless financial modeling backed by statutory certifications. Pro Valuer is a recognized institutional authority in enterprise valuation, business equity appraisals, and intangible asset accounting under the Companies Act 2013, the Income Tax Act 1961, and Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) regulations.

Section 247 Companies Act Registered Valuer Mandates

Under Section 247 of the Companies Act 2013 read with the Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules, 2017, all valuations in respect of any property, stocks, shares, debentures, securities, goodwill, or net worth of a company must be conducted by an IBBI Registered Valuer. ProValuer Commercial executes statutory Section 247 reports for:

  • Mergers, Amalgamations & Demergers: Determination of fair share exchange ratios and swap ratios under Section 230-232 schemes presented before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT).
  • Preferential Allotment & Rights Issues: Valuation of equity shares, Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs), and Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) under Section 42 and Section 62(1)(c).
  • Minority Squeeze-Outs & Buybacks: Independent fair pricing under Section 68 and Section 236 to ensure equitable treatment for minority shareholder blocks.
  • Capital Reduction & Corporate Reorganization: Balance sheet restructuring valuations under Section 66.

Rule 11UA Income Tax Valuation & Angel Tax Defense

The determination of Fair Market Value (FMV) under Rule 11UA of the Income Tax Rules, 1962 is vital for defending transactions against Section 56(2)(viib) additions (commonly known as the Angel Tax) and Section 56(2)(x) deemed income provisions. Pro Valuer applies rigorous, defensible methodologies:

  • Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Method: Establishing robust Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) cost of equity, Terminal Growth Rates (TGR), and free cash flow to firm (FCFF) projections backed by empirical macroeconomic research.
  • Net Asset Value (NAV) Book Method: Reconciling audited balance sheets, substituting book values with statutory fair market values for underlying immovable assets, quoted shares, and unquoted securities under Rule 11UA(2).

FEMA Cross-Border FDI / ODI Pricing Compliance

In cross-border inbound foreign direct investment (FDI) and outbound direct investment (ODI) transactions under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019, non-resident share subscriptions and transfers must comply with strict pricing guidelines. ProValuer provides internationally compliant DCF valuations ensuring that shares issued to non-residents are priced above fair market value, and shares transferred from non-residents to domestic residents are priced below fair market value.

3. Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Resolution Valuation

Under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC), precision in asset valuation forms the bedrock of the corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP) and subsequent liquidation proceedings. Pro Valuer is a trusted partner to Insolvency Professionals (IPs), Resolution Professionals (RPs), Liquidators, and Committees of Creditors (CoC) across NCLT benches nationwide.

Regulation 35 Statutory Determinations: Fair Value & Liquidation Value

In terms of Regulation 35 of the IBBI (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) Regulations, 2016, two independent Registered Valuers must be appointed to determine both the Fair Value and the Liquidation Value of the Corporate Debtor. ProValuer Commercial conducts comprehensive, simultaneous multi-class appraisals across all asset classes:

  • Fair Value in IBC: The estimated realizable value of the assets of the corporate debtor, if they were to be exchanged on the insolvency commencement date between a willing buyer and a willing seller in an arm's length transaction, after proper marketing and where the parties had each acted knowledgeably, prudently, and without compulsion.
  • Liquidation Value in IBC: The estimated realizable value of the assets of the corporate debtor if the corporate debtor were to be liquidated on the insolvency commencement date under auction and distressed distress constraints. Liquidation value governs the minimum payout statutory entitlement of dissenting financial creditors and operational creditors under Section 30(2) of the Code.

Resolution Plan Evaluation & Section 29A Scrutiny

When Resolution Applicants submit competitive resolution plans, the Committee of Creditors relies upon Pro Valuer's independent benchmark valuations to assess commercial viability, enterprise enterprise value, and liquidation recovery floors. If estimates of the two appointed valuers differ significantly, Pro Valuer is frequently appointed as the third valuer under Regulation 35(1)(b) to establish definitive consensus.

4. Plant & Machinery Technical Certification & Industrial Appraisal

Industrial assets, production lines, specialized processing equipment, and heavy machinery require engineering expertise combined with financial valuation discipline. Pro Valuer's Plant & Machinery division is staffed by veteran chartered engineers and IBBI Registered Valuers specializing in industrial technical appraisal.

Technical Inspection, Asset Tagging & Verification

Paper-based depreciation schedules fail to reflect real equipment worth. ProValuer Commercial conducts physical on-site audits, serial number matching, nameplate logging, and physical condition diagnostics across manufacturing plants, thermal and solar power stations, pharmaceutical units, steel rolling mills, textile factories, and chemical refineries.

Engineering Valuation Methodologies

Our industrial engineering valuation practice applies globally recognized standards from the International Valuation Standards Council (IVSC):

  • Depreciated Replacement Cost (DRC) / Modern Equivalent Asset (MEA): Estimating the gross replacement cost of installing modern equivalent machinery with identical utility, followed by systematic deductions for physical wear and tear, functional obsolescence, and economic or external obsolescence.
  • Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Certification: Quantitative engineering assessments determining the remaining operational lifespan of capital assets for leasing contracts, export-import customs clearance, and chartering.
  • Ind AS 36 Asset Impairment Testing: Calculating the Value in Use (VIU) and Fair Value Less Costs of Disposal (FVLCOD) for Cash Generating Units (CGUs) to support corporate statutory auditors during annual financial closings.

5. Government Approved Valuers Practice (Statutory & Judicial)

Statutory authorities, revenue boards, and judicial courts require valuation certificates issued by Government Approved Valuers possessing specific statutory registration under the Wealth Tax Act, 1957. Pro Valuer maintains active registration under Section 34AB with the Principal Chief Commissioner of Income Tax (Pr. CCIT) and provides legally inviolable documentation for high-stakes compliance.

Direct Tax & Capital Gains Defense (Section 50C & Section 55A)

Property transactions in India are heavily scrutinized by the Income Tax Department. ProValuer Commercial prepares statutory valuation reports to defend assessees against unreasonable stamp duty circle rate assessments:

  • Section 50C Stamp Duty Value Objections: Where the circle rate or ready reckoner rate exceeds fair market value due to locational disadvantages, legal disputes, shape irregularities, or physical encumbrances, Pro Valuer prepares evidence-backed reports for reference to the Departmental Valuation Officer (DVO).
  • Section 55A Fair Market Value as of 1st April 2001: For ancestral properties acquired prior to April 1, 2001, calculating the indexed cost of acquisition is critical. Our historical market archives and municipal indices provide certified 2001 FMV reports that withstand scrutiny during scrutiny assessments and appeals before CIT(A) and the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT).
