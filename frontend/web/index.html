Frequently Asked Questions — Property & Asset Valuation

Do you provide property valuation for bank loans?

Yes. ProValuer Commercial prepares certified bank collateral and mortgage valuation reports empanelled and accepted across nationalized PSU banks (SBI, PNB, Canara Bank, Union Bank) and premier private lenders (HDFC, ICICI, Axis Bank) adhering to RBI prudential norms.

Do you provide property valuation for visa purposes?

Yes. Our Government Approved Valuers issue certified property valuation reports and chartered net worth certificates for student visas (US F-1, Canada Study Permit, UK Tier 4, Australia Subclass 500) and permanent residency applications compliant with embassy evidentiary standards.

Do you provide house valuation in Hyderabad?

Yes, we provide physical on-site house and independent villa valuations across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, including Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Gachibowli, Kokapet, Manikonda, and Sainikpuri, utilizing the Land and Building Depreciated Replacement Cost (DRC) and Sales Comparison approaches.

Do you provide flat valuation in Hyderabad?

Yes. We conduct certified apartment and flat valuations across high-rise residential towers in Hitech City, Madhapur, Kondapur, Financial District, Kokapet, Miyapur, and Tellapur, cross-referencing Undivided Share of Land (UDS), carpet area, and current registered market transactions.

Do you provide retrospective valuation?

Yes. We prepare statutory retrospective valuations establishing Fair Market Value (FMV) as of April 1, 2001 under Section 55A of the Income Tax Act for capital gains indexation calculations on ancestral or inherited properties.

Do you provide capital gains valuation?

Yes. Under Section 50C and Section 50CA of the Income Tax Act, our Government Approved Valuers prepare dispute-tested valuation reports to defend assessees when circle rates (SRO values) unreasonably exceed genuine market values due to encumbrances, shape deformities, or litigation.

Are you an IBBI Registered Valuer?

Yes. ProValuer practice partners are Registered Valuers registered with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) under Section 247 of the Companies Act 2013 for Land & Building, Plant & Machinery, and Securities or Financial Assets.

Do you issue Chartered Engineer Certificates?

Yes. Our empanelled Chartered Engineers issue certified valuation and technical certificates for machinery import clearance with Customs (SVB), export compliance (EPCG), plant utility, and international financial proof.

Do you provide mortgage valuation?

Yes. We deliver institutional mortgage valuations establishing Fair Market Value (FMV), Realizable Value (RV), and Distress Sale Value (DSV) to support commercial credit sanction, loan-to-value (LTV) limits, and SARFAESI reserve price compliance.

How long does the process take?

Standard property valuation reports in Hyderabad are delivered within 24 to 48 hours following physical on-site inspection and document verification. Priority expedited dispatch is available for urgent bank sanction and visa interview deadlines.
