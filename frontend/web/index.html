Why is an IBBI Registered Valuer mandatory for corporate transactions in India?

Under Section 247 of the Companies Act, 2013, any valuation required under the Act—including mergers, acquisitions, preferential share allotments, buybacks, or insolvency proceedings—must be conducted solely by a Registered Valuer registered with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI). Valuations executed by non-registered entities are non-compliant and legally invalid before the Ministry of Corporate Affairs and the NCLT.
