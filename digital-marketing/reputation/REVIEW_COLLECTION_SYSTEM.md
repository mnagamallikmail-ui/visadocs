# End-to-End Review Collection System & Reputation Protocol — ProValuer Commercial

**Policy Directive:** Strictly NO fake reviews. Strictly NO incentivized reviews. 100% authentic, verified client feedback collected ethically from genuine property owners, corporate clients, chartered accountants, and visa applicants upon report delivery.

---

## 1. QR Code Destination Strategy & Shortlink Routing

### Technical Architecture
- **Primary Review Routing URL:** `https://www.provaluer.in/review`
- **Dynamic Action:** 
  1. On scanning the physical QR code stamped on the printed hardbound valuation report or clicking the digital link in the final PDF delivery email, the client is directed to `https://www.provaluer.in/review`.
  2. The page renders a high-trust thank-you landing view that immediately presents a 1-click button:  
     **"Review ProValuer Commercial on Google Maps"** (links directly to the Google Business Profile review dialog with place ID: `https://g.page/r/provaluer/review` or direct Search URL).
  3. Clients can also leave feedback for internal quality audits if they encountered any issues during the site inspection.

### Physical Placement on Valuation Dossiers
- **Back of Cover Page / Executive Summary Page:**  
  A neatly styled 1.5-inch security square containing the QR code with the caption:  
  *“How was your valuation experience? Scan to share your verified review on Google.”*

## 3. Review Monitoring & Reputation Governance SOP

1. **Daily SERP & GBP Audit:**  
   The marketing coordinator logs into the Google Business Profile dashboard daily at 10:00 AM IST to inspect incoming reviews.
2. **Mandatory 24-Hour Response SLA:**  
   Every review (whether positive or critical) must receive a professional, courteous, keyword-conscious response within 24 hours using the approved response templates in `GBP_CONTENT_KIT.md`.
3. **Escalation Protocol for Neutral / Critical Reviews (< 4 Stars):**  
   If a client posts less than 4 stars, the Practice Director is alerted within 1 hour. The Director directly dials the client to understand the issue (e.g., delay in municipal plan procurement, clarification on depreciated replacement cost) and provides an immediate resolution.
4. **Compliance Audit:**  
   Monthly audit to ensure zero policy violations, zero review gating, and strict alignment with Google’s Merchant & Business Profile Guidelines.
