## 2. JUDICIAL & REGULATORY ADMISSIBILITY BENCHMARKS

### 2.1 Section 45, Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (Expert Testimony)
Under Indian jurisprudence, an appraisal signed by an unregistered individual constitutes hearsay. Reports signed by our Section 34AB Government Approved Valuers and IBBI Registered Valuers qualify as **certified expert opinion under Section 45 of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872**.
- Admissible in Civil Courts, High Courts, District Courts, and the Supreme Court of India.
- Unchallenged admissibility in Family Court divorce asset division and partition suits.
- Direct locus standi before the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) and National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT).

### 2.2 Rebuttal of Stamp Duty Circle Rates (Section 50C & Section 56(2)(x))
- Circle rates / Ready Reckoner rates are gross statistical approximations and do not account for physical property encumbrances, road width deficits, high-tension lines, low-lying topography, or structural dilapidation.
- Our forensic valuation dossiers present empirical comparative market analysis and CPWD depreciation tables that legally obligate the Assessing Officer (AO) to refer the valuation to the Department Valuation Officer (DVO) or accept our certified FMV.
